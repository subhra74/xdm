# XDM Trademark Policy

"XDM", "Xtreme Download Manager", "XDM Browser Monitor" and the XDM logo (the **XDM Marks**) identify the
official software published by Subhra Das Gupta. The code is open source under the GNU GPL version 3, but
the GPL does not license the name or the logo. This policy says how you may use them.

The aim is simple: anyone who downloads something called XDM should get the official XDM.

## You may, without asking

- **Refer to XDM** by name in articles, reviews, tutorials, videos and comparisons.
- **Say your project is based on XDM**: "MyDownloader, a fork of XDM" or "based on Xtreme Download Manager"
  is fine. The name of your product must not be an XDM Mark.
- **Link to the official project** using the name, e.g. "Get XDM from xtremedownloadmanager.com".
- **Package the unmodified official release** in a Linux distribution or package repository under the XDM
  name, as long as you don't change its functionality (build and packaging patches are fine).
- **Use the name and logo in community material** such as user groups, forums and fan sites, as long as it
  is clear they are not official.

## You may not, without written permission

- **Publish a modified version under an XDM Mark**, in any app store (including Google Play), on any
  website, or anywhere else.
- **Use an XDM Mark, or anything confusingly similar, in your product's name, icon, app ID or store
  listing**: "XDM Pro", "XDM Lite", "Xtreme Downloader" or the XDM logo with a different color all count.
  This covers app IDs (package names) that start with `com.xtremedownloadmanager`.
- **Present your product as official**, made by the XDM project, or endorsed or supported by it.
- **Register a domain name, social media account or store developer name** that contains an XDM Mark.
- **Sell the official XDM**, or publish it with proprietary components, bundled software or tracking added.

## Forks

Forks are welcome; that's the point of open source. Before you publish one:

1. Pick a new name and a new icon.
2. Change the app ID (package name).
3. Remove the XDM logo and the files in `branding/`.
4. Keep the credit to the original project in the About screen, as the license requires.

## Official sources

The official XDM is published only at:

- https://xtremedownloadmanager.com
- https://github.com/subhra74/xdm
- Google Play, by the developer "Subhra Das Gupta", with app ID `com.xtremedownloadmanager.android`
- F-Droid, with app ID `com.xtremedownloadmanager.android`

Any other copy calling itself XDM is unofficial.

## Reporting misuse and asking for permission

To report a copy that misuses the XDM Marks, or to ask for permission for a use this policy doesn't cover,
open an issue at https://github.com/subhra74/xdm/issues or write to the contact address on
https://xtremedownloadmanager.com.

This policy may be updated. The version in the official repository is the current one.
